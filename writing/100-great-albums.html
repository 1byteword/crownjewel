100 great albums

2026.05.25

I've spent the last few years deliberately broadening what I read, watch, and look at — different writers, different eras, different traditions. Music is the one medium where I never bothered.

What I actually listen to is a few dozen songs, on rotation, for years. The shuffle is mine but the well is shallow. I don't sit with an album. I don't follow an artist through their arc. I skip the third track because I always skip the third track, and I've never stopped to figure out why someone put it there.

That's the block I earnestly wish to chip away at. Many of the purported great albums are sickly sweet on a fourth or fifth listen - I feel ill entertaining such false idols (can you honestly listen to a Kendrick album on repeat for the rest of your life?)

A koan to myself — the song I have heard a thousand times, have I heard it once?

The list, as I work through it:

  • Black British Music — Jim Legxacy — 10/10
  • Heaven or Las Vegas — Cocteau Twins — 9/10
  • Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band — The Beatles — 10/10
  • The Downward Spiral — Nine Inch Nails — 5/10
