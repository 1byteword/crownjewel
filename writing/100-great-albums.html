An album at a time

2026.05.25

I keep listening to the same few dozen songs. I’m making myself listen to whole albums for a change.

I’ve been more deliberate about books and films over the last few years. Different people, different periods, things I wouldn’t normally pick. Music somehow escaped that effort. I still skip the same tracks without remembering why I started skipping them.

So I’m working through albums from beginning to end, and giving them a few listens before deciding what I think. The aim is a hundred. The ratings below are mine, provisional, and probably inconsistent.

Some supposedly great records become sickly sweet by the fourth or fifth listen. I don’t want to admire an album just because I’m meant to. Can you honestly listen to a Kendrick album on repeat for the rest of your life?
